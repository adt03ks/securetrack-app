SecureTrack SMS Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: August 24, 2026

These SMS Terms & Conditions govern participation in the SecureTrack Alerts text messaging program.

Program Description

SecureTrack Alerts provides operational equipment-management notifications to authorized users who have voluntarily opted in to receive text messages. Messages may include equipment inspection failures, critical equipment issues, recovery notifications, device status changes, and other equipment-related alerts that may require review or action.

Opt-In

Users enroll by affirmatively opting in through the SecureTrack Notification Settings / SMS Opt-In interface and providing or confirming the mobile number at which they wish to receive messages. Consent to receive SecureTrack SMS messages is voluntary and is not a condition of purchase.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on equipment activity and the notification preferences selected by the user.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply according to the user's mobile carrier and wireless service plan.

Opt-Out

Users may cancel SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP to a SecureTrack text message. After opting out, the user may receive a final confirmation message. Users may also disable SMS notifications through SecureTrack Notification Settings when that option is available.

Help

For assistance, reply HELP to a SecureTrack text message or contact the SecureTrack administrator through the SecureTrack application.

Supported Carriers

Delivery of text messages is subject to carrier availability and network conditions. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

SecureTrack's Privacy Policy explains how information associated with the SMS program is handled. Mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Program Changes

SecureTrack may modify or discontinue the SMS program or these terms when reasonably necessary. Updated terms will be posted at this location with a revised effective date.

Contact

For questions about SecureTrack Alerts, use the support or administrator contact available within the SecureTrack application or reply HELP to a SecureTrack text message.
