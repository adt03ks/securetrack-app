Help center

Help & Support

Find quick guidance for common SecureTrack OP issues. Your organization's approved security operations procedures always take priority over website guidance.

Account and access

If you cannot sign in, confirm your assigned email or username and request assistance from your designated manager or system administrator. Never share your password.

Notification issues

Verify that notifications are allowed in your browser and operating system, that you're signed in, and that your device is online. Notification delivery can vary by device and browser settings.

Device management

For missing inspection records, checkout or return errors, and recovery questions, notify your shift leadership and follow approved device accountability procedures.

Incidents and urgent matters

For emergencies, use your organization's emergency procedures and dispatch channels. Do not rely on a website help request for urgent incident response.

Frequently asked questions

I cannot see the module I need. What should I do?

Access to modules can depend on your assigned role, shift, and organization. Contact your manager or designated system administrator to verify permissions.

Push notifications stopped working. What should I check?

Check device notification permissions, browser permissions, network connectivity, and whether your SecureTrack session is still active. Then contact your administrator if the issue continues.

Can I submit patient records or security incident details here?

No. This public help page is not an approved incident-reporting destination. Use the authenticated workflows or approved organizational channels.

Submit a technical support request

Available to everyone. No SecureTrack login is required. Please do not include passwords, patient information, confidential incident narratives, device secrets, or other sensitive operational details.
