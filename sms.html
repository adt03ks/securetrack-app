SMS Program Information

SecureTrack OP SMS Alerts

SecureTrack OP provides low-volume operational and transactional SMS alerts to authorized managers who voluntarily opt in through the SecureTrack OP Manager Portal. Messages may include failed equipment inspections, critical equipment issues, recovery notifications, device status changes, and other equipment-accountability alerts.

Important: Managers are not automatically enrolled. SMS consent is obtained separately inside Notification Settings and the consent checkbox is unchecked by default. This public page documents the private manager-portal enrollment process for verification and compliance purposes.
Call to Action / Opt-In

How managers enroll in SMS alerts

An authorized manager signs in to the SecureTrack OP Manager Portal using their assigned credentials.
The manager opens Notification Settings.
The manager enters or confirms the mobile number where they want to receive operational alerts.
The manager affirmatively checks the SMS consent checkbox. The checkbox is not pre-selected.
The manager enables SMS notifications, selects the desired operational alert categories, and saves the notification preferences.
SecureTrack OP records the manager's SMS consent status and consent date. The manager may later disable SMS in Notification Settings or reply STOP to a message.
Consent Disclosure

Exact opt-in language

The following disclosure is shown with the SMS consent control:

I agree to receive SecureTrack OP text messages.
I consent to receive operational and transactional equipment-accountability, failed-inspection, safety, critical-equipment-issue, recovery, device-status, and other selected alert text messages from SecureTrack OP at the mobile number provided. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is voluntary and is not a condition of purchase.
Message Content

Example alert

Operational / Transactional
SECURETRACK OP ALERT: CEW-014 failed its required equipment inspection. Checkout has been blocked. Sign in to the SecureTrack OP Manager Portal for details. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

SecureTrack OP SMS messages are used for operational equipment-management events, not advertising or third-party promotional marketing.

Opt-Out

User control

SMS recipients may opt out at any time by replying STOP. Authorized managers may also disable SMS notifications through Notification Settings. Opting out of SMS does not prevent use of other SecureTrack OP functions that do not require text messaging.

Help

Assistance

Recipients may reply HELP to a SecureTrack OP text message for assistance. Additional support is available through the SecureTrack OP application administrator.

Frequency & Rates

Message frequency varies

Message frequency depends on equipment activity and the operational notification categories selected by the manager. Message and data rates may apply according to the recipient's wireless plan.

Privacy

Mobile data protection

Mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
