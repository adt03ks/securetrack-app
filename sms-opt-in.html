Sign In to Complete SMS Opt-In

Consent to receive SecureTrack SMS messages is voluntary and is not a condition of purchase.

SecureTrack SMS messages are operational and non-marketing in nature.

Sensitive personnel details, badge numbers, inspection notes, and patient information are not intended to be transmitted through SMS.
