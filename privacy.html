SecureTrack Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 24, 2026

SecureTrack is an equipment accountability and inventory management application used by authorized personnel. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, protected, and disclosed in connection with SecureTrack, including the SecureTrack SMS notification program.

Information We Collect

SecureTrack may collect information that authorized users provide or generate while using the service, including account or user identifiers, contact information such as a mobile telephone number, equipment and asset identifiers, inspection results, equipment status information, issue reports, transaction history, notification preferences, and related operational records.

How We Use Information

Information collected through SecureTrack is used to operate and support the equipment accountability system, verify and document equipment activity, provide inspection and status information, maintain transaction and audit records, send requested operational notifications, improve system reliability, and support authorized administrative or security-related functions.

SMS Notifications

Authorized users may voluntarily opt in to receive SecureTrack operational text-message alerts. These messages may include equipment inspection failures, critical equipment issues, recovery notifications, device status changes, and other equipment-related alerts requiring review or action.

Message frequency varies based on equipment activity. Message and data rates may apply. Users may reply STOP to opt out of SMS notifications and HELP for assistance. Users may also disable SMS notifications through SecureTrack Notification Settings when that option is available.

Mobile Information and SMS Consent

Mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information Sharing

SecureTrack does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed only as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, maintain, or support the service; to service providers acting on behalf of SecureTrack under appropriate obligations; when required by law; or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or integrity of SecureTrack, its users, or its systems.

Data Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are used to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain operational and audit records, meet applicable legal or administrative requirements, and protect the integrity and security of the system.

User Choices

Users may update applicable notification preferences through SecureTrack. SMS recipients may opt out at any time by replying STOP. Opting out of SMS does not prevent continued use of other SecureTrack functions that do not require text messaging.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any revised policy will be posted at this location with an updated effective date.

Contact

For privacy or SecureTrack SMS questions, contact the SecureTrack administrator through the SecureTrack application or reply HELP to a SecureTrack text message for assistance.
