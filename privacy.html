Privacy Policy

Our approach to safeguarding personnel information, security incidents, device records, and sensitive operational data in facilities that use SecureTrack OP.

Healthcare deployment notice: This policy describes intended data-handling practices and responsibilities. It does not by itself certify SecureTrack OP as HIPAA compliant, establish that every security measure is implemented, or replace an organization-specific vendor assessment, contract, Business Associate Agreement (BAA), or documented security review. Controls and contractual commitments must be verified before live deployment involving protected health information.

1. Scope and organizational roles

SecureTrack OP (the “Platform”) is a security operations software product operated by OmniPresence Branding, LLC (“SecureTrack OP,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). It is designed to support authorized security operations at healthcare facilities and other organizations.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when individuals visit our public website, request support, or access the Platform through an organization. A participating hospital or employer generally determines why its workforce and facility records are entered, who is authorized to use them, and applicable retention requirements. SecureTrack OP acts as a service provider for such organization-controlled records, subject to the applicable agreement and law. For our own website, inquiries, and business administration, we may determine the purposes of processing.

2. Information the Platform may process

Depending on enabled features and organizational configuration, information may include:

  • Account and workforce data: names, work email addresses, usernames, job roles, shift assignments, employment-related identifiers, account status, and authentication metadata.
  • Operational records: duty stations, shift assignments, overtime or time-off requests, training completion, supervisory approvals, and associated timestamps.
  • Incidents and alerts: incident type, descriptions, locations, times, responding personnel, case notes, and related records.
  • Equipment and property records: controlled-device assignments and inspections, condition notes, checkout/return histories, found-property descriptions, and images submitted by authorized users.
  • Support and communications: support requests, correspondence, notification preferences, delivery identifiers, and records needed to address technical issues.
  • Technical data: device/browser details, IP addresses, session and authentication events, system error data, and activity logs where enabled.
  • Potentially sensitive healthcare information: patient-related identifiers or details included in incident narratives or property records where specifically permitted by the deploying organization.

Users should enter only information necessary for legitimate operational purposes and follow their facility's rules for sensitive information.

3. How information is used

We process information as needed to operate the Platform, authenticate authorized users, enforce assigned permissions, document security operations, route approved alerts, troubleshoot support requests, detect misuse, maintain operational continuity, and meet documented legal or contractual requirements. We do not intend the Platform to be used for advertising profiling of patients or staff. We do not sell Platform records or use hospital operational records for third-party advertising.

4. HIPAA and healthcare-sensitive information

A security incident may involve patient information. Whether information is protected health information (“PHI”) under HIPAA depends on the record, the entities involved, and how the Platform is used. The hospital or other covered entity remains responsible for authorizing use, establishing minimum-necessary procedures, and identifying which information may be entered.

Before PHI is entered into a production environment, the parties must assess whether SecureTrack OP acts as a HIPAA business associate, determine whether a BAA is legally required, complete any necessary agreement(s), and confirm that all relevant vendors and product features are appropriately configured and approved. Where HIPAA applies, access, disclosures, safeguards, reporting, and retention must follow the applicable BAA, law, and hospital policy.

The Platform is not a substitute for an electronic health record or a designated clinical documentation system. Do not enter Social Security numbers, full medical records, or other highly sensitive details unless expressly authorized and necessary. Push notifications, email alerts, and support messages should avoid patient identifiers and detailed PHI unless a formally approved, secured workflow supports their transmission.

5. Security safeguards and authorized access

SecureTrack OP is designed for authenticated, role-scoped workflows, including administrative, managerial, team-lead, senior-officer, and officer functions. Access to records should be limited to permissions needed for assigned responsibilities. Depending on the deployment, supporting controls may include database row-level security, server-side authorization, protected connections, system monitoring, and access-event records.

Security controls such as multifactor authentication, encryption key management, centralized audit logging, session management, access recertification, vulnerability assessment, recovery testing, and independent security testing must be evaluated and documented for each production deployment. No Internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. Users must keep credentials confidential, use approved devices, and promptly report suspected unauthorized activity.

6. Disclosures and service providers

We may disclose information to authorized customer personnel, contracted infrastructure and support providers, or when legally required, subject to contracts and applicable law. The Platform currently uses or is integrating services such as Supabase (application backend, database, and authentication) and OneSignal (notifications), and may use other approved delivery and hosting vendors. Specific vendors, data flows, geographic processing locations, and contractual safeguards should be documented in a deployment-specific vendor/subprocessor register.

We do not treat a vendor's general availability or marketing claims as proof that a particular integration is approved to receive PHI. Each PHI-related data flow requires separate assessment and, where required, appropriate agreements.

7. Notifications and connected services

Authorized users may choose or be assigned operational notifications such as incident alerts or workflow updates. Depending on configuration, delivery can involve browser push notifications or email. These channels may process a user identifier, subscription token, notification category, and delivery metadata. Notification contents should use minimal, non-sensitive descriptions by default. Facility administrators should approve alert templates and channel permissions before enabling delivery of sensitive records.

8. Retention, deletion, and backups

Customer-controlled operational records are retained according to the deploying organization's documented retention schedule, contract, legal holds, and applicable law. Different rules may apply to incident reports, personnel records, equipment logs, system logs, and support requests. Records will not necessarily be deleted immediately upon a user's request when preservation is legally or operationally required.

For termination or account closure, the relevant agreement should specify export, return, deletion, backup-expiration timelines, and secure disposal procedures. We do not state a universal deletion period because those schedules must be agreed to and technically verified for each implementation.

9. Access, correction, and privacy requests

For information maintained on behalf of a hospital or employer, users should ordinarily contact their facility's authorized privacy, human resources, or security administrator. We may direct a request to the responsible organization where appropriate. For information we control directly, you may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to identity verification, applicable law, and relevant retention obligations.

10. Security incidents and breach response

Suspected misuse, unauthorized access, data loss, or an information-security incident should be reported promptly to the deploying organization's designated incident-response contact and to SecureTrack OP. We will evaluate reports and coordinate investigation and notifications as required by governing contracts and applicable laws, including HIPAA where applicable. Contractual response and notification deadlines must be established before deployment; this policy does not replace a formal incident response plan.

11. Cookies, sessions, and technical information

The Platform may use authentication sessions, local browser storage, and similar technologies needed for sign-in, security, and user preferences. Supporting services may process network and device data needed to deliver or protect the service. Any nonessential analytics or tracking technologies introduced later should be assessed, documented, and governed by applicable notice or consent requirements. Users should not assume clearing browser storage removes records stored by their organization.

12. Data location and cross-border processing

Service infrastructure and subcontractor processing locations depend on the selected hosting region and vendor configuration. Healthcare organizations should obtain written confirmation of hosting, backup, support-access, and potential cross-border processing arrangements before production rollout. No specific U.S.-only residency guarantee is made by this general policy.

13. Policy updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes in software capabilities, infrastructure, legal requirements, or operating practices. The updated date will appear at the top of the page. Material changes affecting an organization's contracted services or regulated information may also require direct contractual notice or review.
