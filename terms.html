8. SecureTrack Alerts — SMS Terms & Conditions

The following additional terms apply only when the SecureTrack Alerts SMS program is active and offered. Availability may be suspended or limited; enabling push notifications is separate from opting into SMS.

Program description

SecureTrack Alerts may send operational equipment and security-management text notifications to authorized individuals who voluntarily opt in. Examples include failed equipment inspections, critical equipment issues, recovery events, device status changes, and other approved operational notices.

Voluntary opt-in

Where available, users enroll by affirmatively consenting in SecureTrack Notification Settings or another clearly identified SMS opt-in interface and providing or confirming their mobile number. SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase. Users should only enroll a number they are authorized to use.

Frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on operational activity and selected preferences. Message and data rates may apply under the recipient's carrier plan.

STOP and HELP

To unsubscribe from SecureTrack Alerts SMS messages, reply STOP to a program text. A final confirmation may be sent. Users may also disable SMS in Notification Settings when available. For program help, reply HELP to a program text or contact admin@securetrackop.com or (877) 610-9996. STOP/HELP reply handling is subject to the live SMS program being configured and operational.

Delivery and carriers

Wireless carriers are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on carrier support, device and network conditions, and system availability. Do not rely on SMS as the sole mechanism for urgent or emergency response.

Mobile privacy

Mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Necessary service providers may process information to deliver the messages under appropriate arrangements. See our Privacy Policy for additional information.
