Module 01

Personnel Administration

Learn how to maintain personnel records, onboard new officers, manage account access, process campus transfers and employee separations, understand retention reporting, and maintain property accountability.

Lesson 01

Personnel Administration Overview

Personnel Administration is the management workspace used to maintain local personnel, outside officers, employment status, transfers, and post-separation accountability.

The dashboard provides an at-a-glance view of:

  • Active Personnel
  • Outside Officers
  • Open Property Cases
  • Pending Transfers
Manager Standard: Personnel Administration should reflect the employee's current operational status. Update personnel information promptly when authorized employment information changes.
Lesson 02

Active Personnel

Active Personnel contains employees regularly assigned to the local campus.

Personnel cards provide quick access to identifying and operational information such as the employee's name, rank, employee number, current shift, email, telephone number, status, and account-access status.

Available Personnel Actions

  • Send Invite
  • Edit Personnel
  • Qualifications
  • Transfer to Outside Officer
  • Employee Separation
Lesson 03

Add New Officer

Select + Add New Officer to create a new employee's personnel record.

Add New Officer Screenshot add-new-officer.webp

Required Information

  • First and last name
  • Telephone number
  • Email address
  • Employee number
  • Hire date
  • Rank
  • Normal shift

Date of birth, middle initial, nickname, armed qualification, and profile photo may also be recorded when applicable.

Important: Saving a new personnel profile does not automatically activate the employee's SecureTrack login. Account access is handled separately.
Managers may only onboard personnel into shifts they are authorized to supervise.
Lesson 04

Account Access

A personnel profile and SecureTrack login access are intentionally treated as separate steps.

Personnel Record: identifies who the employee is.

Account Access: determines whether the employee can sign in to SecureTrack.

When a personnel card shows Not Set Up, select Send Invite only when the employee is authorized and ready to establish SecureTrack access.

Lesson 05

Edit Personnel

Select Edit Personnel from the employee card to maintain the current personnel record.

Edit Personnel Screenshot edit-personnel.webp

Managers may review or update authorized information including:

  • Officer photo
  • Name information
  • Employee number
  • Telephone number
  • Date of birth
  • Hire date
  • Rank
  • Normal shift
  • Armed qualification
Hire Date Matters: SecureTrack later uses the recorded hire date to calculate Days Employed and Retention Classification during Employee Separation.
The Email Address / Login field is protected from ordinary editing so the personnel record and authentication account do not become inconsistent.
Lesson 06

Qualifications

The Qualifications control is visible within Personnel Administration; however, full manager qualification management is not currently active.

Current Feature Status: Do not rely on the Qualifications button to add, modify, revoke, or verify personnel qualifications until this feature is formally activated.

Armed Qualification is currently maintained through the Add New Officer and Edit Personnel workflows.

Lesson 07

Transfer to Outside Officer

Use Transfer to Outside Officer when the employee remains employed but their permanent home campus changes.

Transfer to Outside Officer Screenshot campus-transfer.webp

The manager confirms:

  • Employee identity
  • Current shift
  • Employee number
  • Current rank
  • New home campus
  • Effective date
  • Optional transfer notes
This changes SecureTrack access. The officer is removed from active local personnel, their local shift assignment is closed, and current local application roles are revoked. Historical records remain preserved.
Situation Correct Action
Employee remains employed but permanently changes campus Transfer to Outside Officer
Employee's employment ends Employee Separation
Lesson 08

Employee Separation

Employee Separation is used to permanently deactivate an employee's operational access after an approved separation.

Employee Separation Form Screenshot employee-separation.webp

Before proceeding, verify:

  • Employee Name
  • Employee Number
  • Current Shift
  • Hire Date
  • Effective Separation Date
  • HR Approval Date
  • Separation Reason
  • Property Return Status
HR Approval Required: Employee Separation should only be finalized after appropriate HR approval has been received.

Lesson 09

Retention Reporting

SecureTrack automatically calculates the employee's tenure during Employee Separation.

Hire Date
↓
Effective Separation Date
↓
SecureTrack Calculates Days Employed
↓
SecureTrack Assigns Retention Classification
Hire Date 09/07/2026
Separation Date 10/19/2026
Days Employed 42
Classification Under 12 Months
Managers do not manually calculate employee retention. SecureTrack calculates the service period from the recorded Hire Date and Effective Separation Date.
Accurate Dates Are Critical: Incorrect hire or separation dates will produce incorrect retention reporting.

Retention Impact

If an employee separates before completing twelve months of service, SecureTrack records the separation as a retention impact.

Later reporting can use this information to analyze early turnover, average days employed, retention trends by shift, and separation patterns.

Retention Classification means employee tenure. It is different from record retention, which refers to how long operational records are preserved.
Lesson 10

Outstanding Property

Outstanding Property is used to maintain accountability when an employee separates but still has unresolved organizational property.

Outstanding Property Screenshot outstanding-property.webp

Items may include:

  • SecureTrack-tracked equipment
  • Keys
  • Access badges
  • Uniforms
  • Other organizational property
Employee separation does not remove unresolved property from management visibility. Outstanding property remains available for follow-up until the accountability process is completed.
Manager Quick Reference

Personnel Administration Decision Guide

Situation Correct Action
New employee joins the department + Add New Officer
Personnel record exists but employee has not established SecureTrack access Send Invite
Name, phone, rank, shift, hire date, or armed status changes Edit Personnel
Qualifications button is visible Feature not active for manager use

Employment ends Employee Separation
Former employee still has unresolved property Outstanding Property
